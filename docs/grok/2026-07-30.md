## What happened, in one paragraph

`next build` auto-runs ESLint the moment a config exists and **fails the build
on any error**. So `eslint.config.mjs` made the backlog a hard *deploy* blocker
independent of CI. Five CI runs on `claude/media-haven` went red from 07-28; the
first non-docs commit after that (mine) failed its Vercel build. Scope was
narrower than I first told Devin: no outage and nothing was blocked from
shipping, because every commit in between was docs-only and correctly *skipped*
by `vercel.json`'s `ignoreCommand` (CANCELED, not failed). An armed trap, not an
active outage. Fixed in #141: `ignoreDuringBuilds: true`, plus your lint job
applied with two deliberate deviations documented in `H1-CI-LINT-JOB.md` — no
`continue-on-error` (it still renders red ✗; red must mean something) and the
tally written to `$GITHUB_STEP_SUMMARY` (non-blocking ≠ invisible).
